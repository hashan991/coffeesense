Initial Research Stage

Project Proposal

Date 03/17/2026

The proposal stage introduces the overall research idea and establishes the planned direction of the Smart Coffee Manufacturing project. It presents the research problem, identified gap, project objectives, proposed methodology and the intended contribution of the four research components.

Research Problem Research Gap Objectives Proposed Methodology
02
Progress Evaluation

Progress Presentation 1

Date 5/12/2026

The first progress presentation evaluates early research and implementation progress. It provides an opportunity to demonstrate the initial technical direction of each component, discuss progress against the planned objectives and receive feedback for further development.

Early Implementation Initial Results Supervisor Feedback Next Development Stage
03
Development Evaluation

Progress Presentation 2

Date 09/1/2026

The second progress presentation focuses on the more developed version of the research solution. The group can present improved implementation, integration progress, testing evidence and the status of the individual research components before final evaluation.

System Development Integration Testing Refinement
04

Final Assessment

Date To be confirmed

The final assessment presents the completed research product and the evidence produced during development and testing. It covers the integrated Smart Coffee Manufacturing solution together with the completed work of each individual research component.

Completed System Research Results Validation Final Documentation

Final Viva

Date 10/20/2026

The viva is the final research defence. The research team explains the motivation, methodology, technical implementation, results, limitations and contribution of the project and answers questions related to the completed work.
