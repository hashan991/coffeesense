Our Team

Four researchers, four components, one integrated system.

Each team member is responsible for a specialized research component within the Smart Coffee Manufacturing project. The components address raw coffee bean quality assessment, coffee powder monitoring, packaging quality inspection, and weather-based sales and quality forecasting.

Together, the four components contribute to a shared research platform that supports automated quality inspection, sensor-based monitoring, decision support, traceability and production planning.

Research Members

Individual research contributions

Each researcher is responsible for one specialized component of the complete Smart Coffee Manufacturing solution.

Component 01

Kumara W.M.H.L

IT22105752

Raw Coffee Bean Quality Assessment

Focuses on AI and sensor-assisted pre-roast quality assessment using bean detection, colour and shape classification, sensor evidence, severity-aware scoring and explainable batch-level decisions.

YOLOv8 Computer Vision Arduino Sensors Quality Scoring
Email IT22105752@my.sliit.lk
Component 02

Bandara A.B.N.N.A

IT22218858

Coffee Powder Quality Monitoring & Intelligent Decision Support

Focuses on Arduino-based moisture, RGB, temperature and humidity monitoring, PASS/WARN/HOLD classification, corrective guidance, batch tracking and reporting.

Arduino Uno DHT22 TCS3200 Decision Support
Email IT22218858@my.sliit.lk
Component 03

Weerarathna N.N.A

IT22249302

Real-Time Coffee Packaging Quality & Leak Detection

Focuses on two-stage YOLO-based seal inspection together with an Arduino-controlled mechanical load-response prototype for evaluating visible sealing defects and potential packaging integrity problems.

YOLO11 Load Cell HX711 Seal Inspection
Email IT22249302@my.sliit.lk
Component 04

Gunawardhana W.A.C.T.D

IT22088932

Weather-Based Coffee Sales & Quality Forecasting

Focuses on historical sales and weather data, Random Forest regression and classification, twelve-month forecasting, quality prediction and SHAP-based explainability for production planning.

Email IT22088932@my.sliit.lk
