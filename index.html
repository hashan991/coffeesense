About the Project

Intelligent quality control across the coffee manufacturing process.

Smart Coffee Manufacturing is an integrated research project designed to support automated and data-driven quality control across multiple stages of coffee manufacturing.

The system combines sensor monitoring, computer vision, machine learning, explainable decision support, reporting and forecasting to improve consistency, traceability and production planning from raw coffee beans through packaging.
